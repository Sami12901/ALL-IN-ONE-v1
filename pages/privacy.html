Privacy Policy

Complete Privacy Commitment

Your privacy is our highest priority. Because ALL IN ONE is a fully static website, no data you input, upload, or process ever leaves your device. There are no backend database servers, API logs, or user tracking systems.

How Data is Handled

  • Text and Inputs: Word counts, JSON inputs, and base64 strings are processed purely in the browser's temporary memory space.
  • Files and Media: When uploading photos to crop, compress, or generate QR codes, the browser Canvas API processes them locally. No files are uploaded to any server.
  • Local Storage: Feature preferences like Dark Theme and your saved Favorites are cached on your machine using standard browser localStorage.

Cookies and Third-Party Links

ALL IN ONE does not place tracking cookies. However, some external documentation links (like GitHub repositories or external guides) may operate under separate privacy guidelines. We encourage reviewing their respective policies.
