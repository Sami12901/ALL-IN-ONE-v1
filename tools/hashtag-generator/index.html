Hashtag Strategy & Algorithm Best Practices

The Ladder Strategy

Don't only target giant hashtags (>10M posts) where your content disappears in seconds. Mix Popular (high discovery), Medium (niche industry terms), and Micro (<50k posts) community tags to climb the Explore page.

Optimal Platform Limits

Instagram: Up to 30 allowed (3–5 targeted tags recommended in 2026). TikTok: 3–6 trending hashtags. LinkedIn: 3–5 professional hashtags. Twitter / X: 1–2 focused tags.

CamelCase for Accessibility

Writing tags in #CamelCase (e.g. #WebDevelopment instead of #webdevelopment) enables screen readers used by visually impaired users to pronounce words properly.
