Key Features & Technical Capabilities

100% Client-Side Privacy

Your video is processed strictly inside your browser memory. No videos or frames are uploaded to any external cloud server.

Exemplar Texture Synthesis

Criminisi exemplar-based inpainting samples texture patches from surrounding imagery to realistically reconstruct background details.

Dual Selection (Brush & Box)

Paint irregular contours with the Freehand Brush or enclose rectangular regions with 8 resize handles for maximum control.

Synchronized Audio Stream

Original audio, sound effects, and voice tracks are captured and merged into the clean output WebM video file.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What kinds of objects can be removed?

You can remove unwanted people, photobombers, boom microphones, power lines, wires, trash, or stationary obstacles from your video clips.

How does the Freehand Brush work?

Select "Freehand Brush", adjust the brush size slider, and paint directly over the target object. You can use Undo and Eraser mode as needed.

Can I process high frame rate videos?

Yes. You can choose from 15 FPS (fast draft), 24 FPS (cinematic), 30 FPS (standard), and 60 FPS (high fidelity) depending on your speed needs.

Is there any software installation required?

No. Video Object Remover works directly in any modern desktop or mobile browser without plugins, extensions, or server software.
