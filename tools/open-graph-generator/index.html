Open Graph & Social Metadata Master Guide

Master how modern social media platforms, messaging applications, and search crawlers parse rich media cards.

Optimal Image Dimension: 1200 × 630 px

Facebook, Twitter / X, and LinkedIn all standardize on a 1.91:1 aspect ratio. Providing an image of exactly 1200×630px guarantees crisp rendering on high-DPI Retina screens without auto-cropping or blurry letterboxing.

Character Limits & Truncation

Keep og:title between 40 and 60 characters to prevent ellipsis truncation across mobile timelines. Aim for 100 to 160 characters for og:description to balance descriptive clarity with concise readability.

Impact on Organic CTR & Engagement

Pages equipped with complete Open Graph & Twitter Card tags experience up to 40% higher click-through rates when shared on X, LinkedIn, Discord, and Slack compared to generic raw links.

Crawler Cache Invalidation

Social platforms cache preview tags aggressively for days. When updating meta tags on existing URLs, force refresh using official crawlers: Facebook Sharing Debugger, LinkedIn Post Inspector, and Twitter Card Validator.
