Understanding Experimental Error Accuracy Levels

In experimental physics, chemistry, and engineering laboratories, the magnitude of percentage error evaluates measurement reliability:

< 1% (Excellent)

Exceptional precision. Common in calibrated electronic or laser sensor apparatus.

< 5% (Good)

Standard acceptable student laboratory tolerance with slight human or instrument error.

< 10% (Moderate)

Noticeable deviation. Usually attributed to friction, air resistance, or heat loss.

≥ 10% (High Error)

Significant discrepancy. Review experimental procedure, calibration, or calculation errors.
