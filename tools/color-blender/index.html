Understanding Color Interpolation Spaces

The choice of color space dramatically influences how colors mix in digital design:

CIE LCh (Recommended)

Separates Lightness, Chroma (vividness), and Hue. Traverses the color wheel smoothly while preserving luminance.

CIE Lab

Perceptually uniform Cartesian space modeled after human vision. Excellent for even lighting and smooth contrast progression.

HSL

Travels along cylindrical hue angles. Can generate vibrant rainbow intermediate hues between complementary colors.

sRGB & Linear RGB

Traditional digital display blending. Between distant hues (e.g. blue and yellow), RGB often suffers from a desaturated gray or muddy valley.
