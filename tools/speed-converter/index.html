Velocity Conversion Standards

1 km/h = 0.621371 mph = 0.277778 m/s
Knots (kn): Nautical miles per hour (1 kn = 1.852 km/h = 1.15078 mph). Used in aviation & maritime navigation.
Mach 1 (Sound): Speed of sound in air at 20°C (343 m/s = 1,234.8 km/h = 767.27 mph).
Speed of Light (c): Universal constant in vacuum: 299,792,458 m/s (approx. 1.079 billion km/h).
