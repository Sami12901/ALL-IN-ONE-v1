Entropy & Brute Force Guidelines

Entropy is measured as $E = L \times \log_2(R)$, where $L$ is length and $R$ is character variety.

  • < 35 bits: Vulnerable to near-instantaneous GPU cracking.
  • 50 - 70 bits: Standard safety for low-value personal accounts.
  • 80+ bits: Military-grade resistance against cluster brute force.
