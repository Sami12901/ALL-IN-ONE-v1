The Connoisseur / Collector

Erudite, provenance-obsessed patron who treats acquisitions as curated investments and works of art.

Core Motivation: Rarity, complication mastery, museum provenance.
Top Products: Haute Horology, rare gemstones, bespoke commissions.
Salon Format: Private Vault Complication Showcase with archival loupe examination.
Cadence: 4–6 weeks; confidential and intellectually engaging.
Persona 2 $50k - $150k+ / yr

The Status Trendsetter

Charismatic, culturally visible tastemaker thriving on priority runway access, viral drops, and red carpet distinction.

Core Motivation: Cultural prominence, runway exclusivity, instant recognition.
Top Products: Runway Ready-to-Wear, limited-edition It-Bags, gala couture.
Salon Format: VIP Runway Champagne Preview & Styling Suite with photo-ready setting.
Cadence: Bi-weekly or instant alerts on runway allocations via WhatsApp.
Persona 3 $50k - $120k+ / yr

The Quiet Luxury Loyalist

Discreet stealth-wealth patron dedicated to tactile perfection, pure vicuña, zero visible logos, and timeless longevity.

Core Motivation: Understated refinement, tactile sensory mastery, supreme comfort.
Top Products: Pure vicuña knitwear, bespoke tailoring, unbranded calfskin bags.
Salon Format: In-Suite Trunk Fitting & Fabric Masterclass with zero sales pressure.
Cadence: Seasonal or quarterly; handwritten stationery correspondence.
Persona 4 $5k - $25k+ / yr

The Aspirational Gifter

Generous, celebratory buyer seeking foolproof brand prestige for milestone anniversaries, festive holidays, and corporate tokens.

Core Motivation: Milestone celebration, recipient delight, immaculate packaging.
Top Products: Small leather goods, signature silk scarves, haute parfumerie.
Salon Format: Holiday Champagne Gifting Salon with on-site calligraphy & courier.
Cadence: Calendar-driven (3–4x yearly before key gifting holidays).
