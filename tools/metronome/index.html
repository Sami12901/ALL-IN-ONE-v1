Italian Term BPM Range Literal Meaning Musical Feel & Application
Larghissimo / Grave 30 – 39 Very slow and solemn Extremely slow, dark, sustained funeral marches or symphonic intros.

Keyboard Shortcuts & Professional Practice Techniques

Essential Keyboard Shortcuts

  • Space : Start / Stop Metronome
  • T : Tap Tempo calculation
  • ↑ / ↓ : Adjust tempo ±1 BPM
  • Shift + ↑ / ↓ : Adjust tempo ±5 BPM
  • M : Toggle Audio Mute

The Gap Metronome Method

Click individual beat dots to mute Beats 2, 3, or 4. Practicing against only Beat 1 tests your internal rhythmic clock and prevents relying on the click for every note.

Speed Trainer Discipline

Start difficult etudes or scale runs at 60% of goal speed. Enable the Tempo Speed Trainer with +2 BPM every 4 bars. If tension creeps into your hands, dial back 5 BPM and stabilize before advancing.
