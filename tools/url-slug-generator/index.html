Quick SEO Guidelines

  • Keep it under 50 characters: Short URLs are easier to index, share, and achieve higher click-through rates.
  • Hyphens over underscores: Google explicitly treats hyphens (-) as word separators, while underscores (_) run words together.
  • Remove stop words: Eliminating words like "a", "the", and "of" prevents permalink bloat and focuses keyword relevance.
