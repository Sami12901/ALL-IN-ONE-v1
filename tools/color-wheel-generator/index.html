Color Theory & Harmony Archetypes

Color harmonies define geometric relationships on the chromatic color wheel that human eyes perceive as natural, engaging, and aesthetically balanced:

Complementary (180°)

Colors directly opposite each other on the wheel. Yields maximum visual contrast and energy; excellent for call-to-action buttons.

Analogous (±30°)

Adjacent hues sitting side-by-side. Creates serene, comfortable, nature-inspired transitions often seen in cohesive brand systems.

Triadic (120°)

Three hues forming an equilateral triangle. Offers high vibrancy and diversity while preserving optical harmony.

Split-Complementary

Uses the base color plus the two colors flanking its complement. Delivers strong contrast without the visual tension of pure complements.
